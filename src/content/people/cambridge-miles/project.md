When online or scrolling through social media, you’ve most likely seen a meme, which is an image (animated or not), often accompanied by text, with the intent of creating a humorous message related to a specific topic or theme. Memes have helped me express myself in ways that are more effective than just words, whether it’s an image that I want people to react to or an image showing my reaction. This interest has led me to start my social media page, **Miles Makes Memes**. The website prototype designed in Figma acts as an archive for the memes that I have created and shared so far. The site also includes a tutorial that people can follow to make their own memes and show how to properly apply them to conversations. I’ve had a lot of fun creating memes that others could relate to, and as a result, have grown closer to others by persuading them to use memes as a form of communication.
