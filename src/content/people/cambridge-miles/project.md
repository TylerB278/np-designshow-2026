If you’ve been online or you were scrolling through Instagram or Twitter, you’ve most likely seen a meme, which is an image (animated or not), often accompanied by text, with the intent of creating a humorous piece of media. Memes have helped me express myself in ways that are more effective than just words, whether it’s an image that I want people to react to or an image showing my reaction. This interest has led me to start my social media page, Miles Makes Memes. Along with this, I designed a website within Figma that acts as an archive for the memes that I have made on the social media platform, as well as a tutorial that people can follow to make their own memes and how to properly apply them to conversations. I’ve had a lot of fun with this project, creating memes that others could relate to, and as a result, growing closer to others and persuading them to want to use memes as a form of communication.
