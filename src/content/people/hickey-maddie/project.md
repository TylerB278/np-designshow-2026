How do you express yourself?  

**Expressive Lines** embraces and explores abstraction through hand drawn art, motion, form, texture, color, gesture and movement. Lines are active by nature, made by hand or digitally or a combination of both, and are a result of action, a path created. Expressive lines dig deeper. I have created lines with markers, pens, a toothbrush, and even strange objects like a battery or a charger; lines can be made out of anything! This project is my own form of expression that I have carried with me for years, and have used to help express my emotions through a visual medium. Expressive Lines consists of a book and motion graphics that explore the creative process on how lines are made and how they could be made, and the variation and creative possibilities that can bloom from a single stroke of a marker or pen.
